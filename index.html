Controls

When off, you cannot drag or flick the wheel with the mouse. Keyboard and double-click spin still work if those options are on. When off, clicking or dragging during a spin cannot stop it — the spin always runs to the end. When on, a flick that is fast enough starts a normal full-force timed spin (Spin duration) instead of matching how hard you threw it. When this wheel is set to spin a different wheel: if on, multi-spin waits for that wheel (and its queue) to finish before continuing this wheel’s queue. If off, this wheel keeps spinning and only adds to the other wheel’s queue. When unlocked, drag the triangle around the wheel to choose where winners are read. Snaps to 0° / 90° / 180° / 270° (top / right / bottom / left).
